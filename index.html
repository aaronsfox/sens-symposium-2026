SENS Research Symposium 2026

October 9th 2026

Welcome to the School of Exercise and Nutrition Sciences 2026 Research Symposium. Our program each year continues to showcase the wide variety and quality of research being conducted by PhD, Honours, Graduate Certificate, Masters and undergraduate students from the school. This years' program includes a variety of mixed theme sessions to give students an opportunity to present to a varied audience, and for attendees to learn about the multi-disciplinary research happening in the school. As in recent years, we have included a 10-minute presenter-audience networking period at the end of sessions to allow for further discussion with presenters about their work.
